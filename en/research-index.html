Background research — the places and the periods

Reports on the towns, regions and periods in which the family lived. They are not about the family itself but about the world around it — what people ate, what they wore, what they lived on, and what befell the communities they belonged to. Every fact carries a source.

How to read these reports. They are working documents, not book chapters. We preferred to record what we do not know as well: [general background] marks a fact true of the region but not documented for our own family, and ◐ marks a reasonable supposition with no document behind it. Each report ends with its sources and with a list of known gaps.
Thirteen of the twenty reports have been translated. The seven still available in Hebrew only are listed below in their own section, and each links to the Hebrew page.
