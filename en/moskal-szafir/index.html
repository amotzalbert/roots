Mother's side

Moskal–Schafir

The Schafir family in Wojsławice, in the Chełm district, and behind it — villages on the river Bug, and also a town in Austrian Galicia. The Moskal family, owners of a shop and a beer house in the town. The two branches meet in David Moskal and Malka Schafir.

The best-documented starting point: Aron and Perla Bukszpan of the village of Dorohusk, born around 1760 — the deepest generation we have been able to document in the whole research, from a death act of 1833. Open in the tree →
