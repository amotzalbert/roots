Albert–Rywenbajrych

The Alpert–Albert family of Warsaw, and the Rywenbajrych family of Iwaniska and Opatów — a Hasidic town that was the seat of R. Avraham Yehoshua Heschel, the "Ohev Yisrael". Chaim Albert and Chana Rywenbajrych married and founded the Israeli branch of the family.
